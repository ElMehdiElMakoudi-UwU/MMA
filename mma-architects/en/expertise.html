Architecture that answers an investor's questions.

You can start with a feasibility study alone, then decide. You approve each stage before the next begins.

Six stages, one team.

1

Feasibility

We read the planning rules and test what the plot can really hold, before you commit further.

2

Concept

Layouts, volumes and 3D views you can compare and react to.

3

Developed design

The chosen concept, measured and coordinated: plans, sections, façades, materials.

4

Building permit

We prepare and file the application, then follow it with the authorities.

5

Contractors

Construction documents and comparable quotes, with a clear recommendation.

6

Site supervision

Regular visits and reports through to handover.
