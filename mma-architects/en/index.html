The decisions that make or lose the most money are taken before the first wall goes up. What the plot allows. What the building can hold. What it will feel like to live in, stay in or buy. We make those decisions visible, while they can still change.

Case study · Tangier

4,604m²

The rules take most of it

A 4,604 m² plot looked like an easy win. Once the planning rules were applied, only 1,353 m² remained buildable. This is where most projects lose money, before anyone notices.

We test the volume

Base, main body and upper levels are tested against the limits until the building earns its height on that exact plot.

We draw every floor to work

Apartment by apartment, the floor plate is drawn so that the building you can sell or rent matches the building you can build.

A restricted plot becomes an address

Two minutes on foot from Tanger Ville station, the constraint has turned into the building's identity.
